# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss the work and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, limit participation, or ban repeat offenders. Report concerns privately to david@niessen.dev. Reports will be reviewed with discretion; maintainers cannot promise absolute confidentiality where legal obligations apply. This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project spaces.
